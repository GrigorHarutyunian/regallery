Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Re Gallery ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information in connection with the Re Gallery Shopify application (the "App"), and any related websites, dashboards, or support services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy applies to merchants who install the App on their Shopify store ("Merchant," "you") and, where applicable, to visitors and customers of a Merchant's store who interact with galleries created through the App.

By installing, accessing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described here, please do not install or use the App.

1. Introduction

Re Gallery is a Shopify application that enables Merchants to build and manage image galleries, video galleries, lookbooks, and product showcase displays; organize and customize visual content; apply AI-powered media enhancements; and review gallery engagement analytics. To provide these features, the App interacts with certain data made available through the Shopify platform, as well as data generated through use of the App itself.

We are committed to handling personal data responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"), and Shopify's own privacy and API requirements for partner applications.

This Privacy Policy is designed to be read alongside Shopify's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which govern the Shopify platform independently of the App.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • "Merchant" means the Shopify store owner or authorized user who installs and configures the App.
  • "Customer" or "Visitor" means an end user who browses or interacts with a Merchant's storefront, including any gallery, lookbook, or showcase created using the App.
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
  • "Service Provider" or "Sub-processor" means a third-party vendor engaged by us to support the operation of the App.
  • "Shopify APIs" means the Admin API, Storefront API, and related interfaces through which the App reads or writes store data with Merchant authorization.

3. Information We Collect

We collect information in three general categories: (a) information obtained from Shopify with the Merchant's authorization, (b) information generated automatically through use of the App, and (c) information the Merchant or its authorized users may provide to us directly, such as through support requests. We do not require Customers or store Visitors to create an account with us, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data directly from Customers outside of what is necessary to render gallery content and record aggregate engagement metrics described below.

We collect only the categories of data reasonably necessary to deliver the features a Merchant has enabled, consistent with Shopify's data minimization expectations for App Store listings.

4. Information Collected from Shopify

When a Merchant installs the App and grants the relevant permissions, we may access the following data through Shopify's APIs:

Store Information

  • Store name
  • Store URL / domain
  • Shopify subscription plan
  • Merchant account email address

Product Information

  • Product titles
  • Product images and media
  • Product descriptions
  • Product collections and associated metadata

We request only the access scopes required for the specific features a Merchant has enabled within the App. We do not request or use permissions unrelated to gallery creation, media display, customization, or the analytics functions described in this Policy. Merchants can review the specific scopes requested by the App on the App's permission screen at the time of installation and within the Shopify admin.

5. Information Collected Automatically

Usage Information

  • App configuration and settings
  • Gallery layout and configuration data
  • Installation date and uninstallation date
  • Feature usage analytics (e.g., which gallery types or tools are used)
  • Browser type and version

    18. Shopify Mandatory Webhooks Compliance

    In accordance with Shopify's requirements for App Store applications, we implement and respond to Shopify's mandatory GDPR/compliance webhooks, including:

    • customers/data_request — when a store's Customer requests access to their data, we will provide any relevant data held by the App to the Merchant or Shopify as required;
    • customers/redact — when a request is received to delete a Customer's data, we will delete any corresponding Personal Data held by the App, subject to applicable legal retention requirements;
    • shop/redact — 48 hours after a Merchant uninstalls the App (or as otherwise specified by Shopify), we will delete or anonymize the Merchant's store data held by the App, subject to the retention exceptions described in Section 12.

    These webhook processes operate independently of, and in addition to, the direct deletion request process described in Section 17.
